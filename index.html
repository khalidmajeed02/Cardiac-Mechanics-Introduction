SELECTED STAGE

Atrial Systole

(Late ventricular diastole)
▶ Triggered by The sinoatrial node — the heart's pacemaker — initiates a new cardiac cycle, sending a depolarisation wave across both atria.

Passive filling has brought the ventricles to roughly 75% of their end-diastolic volume, but atrial contraction is needed to complete filling. The sinoatrial node fires, atrial pressure rises above ventricular diastolic pressure, and the remaining volume is driven through the open AV valves. This stage ends when ventricular depolarisation triggers myocardial contraction, ventricular pressure rises above atrial pressure, and the AV valves close.

CURRENT STEP

Atrial Systole

Both atria contract simultaneously, propelling blood through the open AV valves and completing filling to end-diastolic volume (EDV). The semilunar valves are closed throughout — no blood enters the great vessels during this phase.

👁 Watch the animation Watch the pulsing blue and red arrows — they show blood flowing simultaneously from both atria into the ventricles. Notice the arrows intensify as atrial contraction peaks, then fade as the stage ends.
